## Size your launch
- **Launch tier S (minimal) · M (targeted) · L (multi-channel) · XL (full GTM):** L
- **Justification reach + revenue impact + what silence would risk:** The feature targets a broad segment of frequent viewers across key discovery surfaces. It can directly improve content-start conversion and engagement, while limited exposure could mean missing a meaningful opportunity to reduce browsing drop-off.
- **Is this a launch or a release? does it need go-to-market, or can it just ship?:** Launch — it needs a lightweight GTM because the feature changes how users discover content and its success depends on users noticing and engaging with it. It can be supported with in-product messaging rather than a large external campaign.

- **Channel 1 owned / earned / paid, and why it reaches your audience:** Owned in app: Feature the Hidden Gem Badge directly on the homepage/content discovery experience where frequent viewers are already browsing.
- **Channel 2 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Earned: Social Media / Word of Mouth : Encourage viewers to share and recommend their “Hidden Gem” discoveries, generating organic reach and curiosity.
- **Channel 3 owned / earned / paid, and why:** Paid Social Media Ads: Target frequent viewers with short ads showcasing Hidden Gem titles and the value of discovering something worth watching quickly.

- **Success metrics 2 to 3 metrics that match your GTM goal:** Conversion rate from title impression → play; Hidden Gem Badge CTR; 30+ minute session rate
- **Bad signal to watch for e.g. high reach, zero signups = message-market mismatch:** High badge impressions but low CTR/play conversion = the badge isn’t compelling or users don’t trust the recommendation.
- **Most likely post-launch decision double-down · iterate · pivot · deprioritize, and what would trigger it:** Iterate — if engagement is strong but play conversion remains low, test the badge messaging, placement, or title selection.
