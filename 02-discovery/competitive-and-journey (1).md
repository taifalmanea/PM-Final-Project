# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Persona
- **Role:** A long-term, frequent StreamLine viewer who values discovering good content.
- **Goal:** Find something genuinely worth watching quickly, without losing the evening to browsing.
- **Friction:** Overwhelmed by a 15,000-title catalog; after 20 minutes of scrolling, still nothing chosen.

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** Physical DVDs or another alternative outside StreamLine. (If your Executive Summary names other tools, such as rival apps or review sites, add them here.)
- **The process:** Opens StreamLine intending to watch something good.
Scrolls rows and categories looking for a title that stands out.
Keeps browsing past 20 minutes without committing.
Gives up and closes StreamLine.
Reaches for a DVD or other alternative instead.
- **Core frustration:** The 20-minute mark, when effort has been spent and there's still nothing to watch.
- **The evidence:** UXR-01: users scroll for 20 minutes, can't find anything they want, close StreamLine, and sometimes turn to a DVD.

## Future-state journey map
1. Open: Opens to a curated shortlist → skips the 15,000-title wall and feels calm.
2. Explore: Browses collections with curator notes → trusts picks over generic rows.
3. Decide: Checks a "why watch" card → picks quickly, ending the 20-minute scroll.
4. Return: Watches, rates, saves → fresh picks keep them in StreamLine, not a DVD.

## Competitive advantages
Speed: Curated shortlist appears instantly → no hunting through shelves or apps.
Breadth with trust: Curators draw from 15,000 titles → far richer than a personal DVD shelf.
Freshness and continuity: Picks refresh and learn from ratings → the DVD shelf never evolves.
