# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
If StreamLine does nothing for 12 months, it risks losing quality-focused viewers to competitors that make it easier to discover compelling content, weakening engagement, retention, and its position as a streaming destination.

## Moment of misery
The user is currently forced to leave StreamLine and search Google, social media, or competitor platforms for reviews and recommendations to figure out what is actually worth watching.

## Problem hook
StreamLine’s 15,000-title catalog is becoming a liability: users struggle to find content worth watching, pushing quality-focused viewers to competitors.

## Value proposition
For For quality-focused viewers,, we will Spotlight turns StreamLine’s overwhelming catalog into a curated selection of high-quality content,  because making it easier to discover what is worth watching before users look elsewhere. Launching now is urgent because engagement has already plateaued and continued friction risks further user loss..

## Cold-read self-review
StreamLine’s business risk is high enough to justify a new initiative because continued discovery friction can lead to declining engagement, retention pressure, and users turning to competitors. The misery is systemic rather than an edge case: with 15,000 titles, users can consistently struggle to identify what is worth watching and leave the platform to search elsewhere. Spotlight directly addresses this obstacle by replacing overwhelming choice with curated, quality-focused discovery.
