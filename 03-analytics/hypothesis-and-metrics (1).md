## Evidence
- **Qualitative (M2):** “I open the app, scroll for like twenty minutes, and close it without watching anything. There's 15,000 titles but nothing I actually want.”
- **Quantitative (M3):** Only 11% of homepage visitors reach a 30+ minute session, down from 19% six months ago. In addition, Content searched then played fell from 41% to 34%.

## Persona & problem
- **Role:** Quality-Seeking Heavy Viewer
- **Goal:** Find something genuinely worth watching quickly.
- **Friction:** Spends ~20 minutes browsing without finding something worth watching, then abandons StreamLine.
- **Problem you are solving:** Users struggle to turn StreamLine’s large content library into a confident viewing choice, leading to browsing without playback and eventual abandonment.

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** Increase the number of users who move from browsing to sustained viewing, reducing abandoned sessions and supporting engagement and retention.
- **Primary success metric:** 30+ minute session rate — target a 20% relative increase, from 11% to 13.2%+.
- **Guardrail metric:** Start Playing rate — currently 29%. It should not materially decline, ensuring improved discovery does not come at the expense of users actually starting content.
- **Decision window:** 4–6 weeks after launch, using a stable weekly trend. Scale if the success metric improves by ≥20% relative while the guardrail remains stable; otherwise pivot or stop.
